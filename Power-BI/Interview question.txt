Here are **Power BI interview questions tailored fBeginner Level (Basics of Power BI)

1. What is Power BI and how is it used in data analysis?
2. What are the main components of Power BI?
3. Difference between Power BI Desktop, Service, and Mobile?
4. What is a dataset, report, and dashboard in Power BI?
5. What are different data sources you can connect to in Power BI?
6. What is Power Query used for?
7. What is the difference between **Import mode and DirectQuery**?
8. What are filters in Power BI (visual-level, page-level, report-level)?
9. What is a relationship in Power BI data model?
10. What is data modeling?

--- Intermediate Level (Data Analysis Focus)

1. What is DAX and why is it important?
2. Difference between **calculated column vs measure**?
3. What are common DAX functions you have used? (SUM, CALCULATE, FILTER, etc.)
4. How do you handle missing or null values in Power BI?
5. What is data cleaning in Power Query?
6. How do you remove duplicates in Power BI?
7. What is row context and filter context in DAX?
8. How do you optimize a slow Power BI report?
9. What is a star schema and why is it preferred?
10. How do you join tables in Power BI?

--- Advanced Level (Real Job Scenarios)

1. How would you design a dashboard for business stakeholders?
2. How do you decide which KPIs to include in a report?
3. How do you handle large datasets efficiently in Power BI?
4. What are performance tuning techniques in Power BI?
5. Explain CALCULATE function with a real example.
6. How do you implement time intelligence (YTD, MTD, YoY)?
7. How would you validate data accuracy in a report?
8. What is incremental refresh and when do you use it?
9. How do you handle inconsistent date formats in raw data?
10. Describe a Power BI project you worked on end-to-end.
